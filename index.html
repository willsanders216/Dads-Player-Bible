The World

Regions beyond the inner boundary exist, but crossing between them isn't simple — something stops most people from traveling far past their own borders. Somehow, pirates have found a way around that. As far as the crew's seen, the inner boundary is the only place where magic actually works.

the inner boundary

The region the crew's whole journey has taken place in — soaked in magic, unlike anywhere else they've heard of. Tap a place below for what's known about it.

Safire

Billy's home region — a fishing culture built around a fear of the sea witches' judgment.

Cult of the Elitists

Orin's home region — a culture that believes itself the master race. That belief isn't baseless pride, though: a hidden library there revealed a darker history — before Orin's people evolved into what they are now, they were treated as vermin, hunted for sport and forced to dance for coin just to survive. Discovering that truth is exactly what pushed the culture to declare itself the master race, as if to bury the memory of what they used to be.

General knowledge any Inner Boundary local would have:

  • Aric — Head of the Navy. The Navy is the governing authority across the whole Inner Boundary, not just one region.
  • The Crow's Nest — the capital. Seat of the King, and where the Navy is based.
  • The Kraken is rumored to exist — no one's ever been proven to have lived to tell the tale of seeing one.
  • Plums don't exist here — closest thing is a sour little fruit called a "duskberry."
  • Piracy is outlawed by the Navy — common knowledge, not a secret or rumor.
  • Broccoli is just called "trees" by pretty much everyone, kids included.
  • Sea witches once held real authority over magic use — including wiping out teleportation magic entirely — but haven't held any power themselves since the pirate faction enslaved them.
  • Michael Cera is a famous theatre playwright — most taverns have at least one of his plays half-memorized by the regulars.
  • The sky is commonly called "the ceiling" or "the roof" — no one's ever made it far enough up to prove otherwise.
  • Rumor going around: an old conman — the same one who once lured in some of the Inner Boundary's most influential figures, before disappearing off the face of the earth — is now trying to lure pirates into some kind of trap.
  • Others believe that same conman actually found a promised land, and is trying to bring other pirates there to join him.

Billy Buckeye — Paladin 5, Wood Elf

AC 17 · HP 39 · Speed 35 ft · Initiative +2

STR 19 (+4) · DEX 14 (+2) · CON 12 (+1) · INT 5 (−3) · WIS 16 (+3) · CHA 14 (+2)

Key Features. Oath of the Open Seas (Paladin subclass), Lay on Hands (25 HP healing pool per long rest), Channel Divinity — Divine Sense, Fury of the Tides, Marine Layer — Extra Attack, immune to being put to sleep by magic.

Passive Perception 16 · Passive Insight 16 · Passive Investigation 7

Background

Grew up in Amuli, a fishing village in Safire — a place where anyone who can't contribute is believed to be cast out to sea for the ocean witches to judge. Met Marilda young; she's the one who got him past his fear of the water. They married, had a son, Tide. Years ago, Marilda and Tide both vanished without a trace. Billy set out to sea to find them, and eventually did — at Crabshack, though Tide had aged decades in what felt like only months to him.

On the Ship

Captain of the crew, co-captaining alongside Mercy Murdersome.

With Tide

Reuniting with Tide hasn't been easy — the two are still finding their footing, and Tide doesn't share everything with him. Tide was taken again at the end of the stronghold heist, and strangely, nobody else in the crew seems to remember he ever existed — only Billy does.

Leader. Aric.

Reputation. In practice, feels more like they make the rules up as they go.

Reputation. By far the biggest and most dangerous pirate community out there.

Aric's Shield of Promise

Shield, rare (requires attunement)

Currently — Level 5. 3 Promise Charges (next increase at Level 9).

View Billy's Stat Block

Billy Buckeye — Paladin 5, Wood Elf

AC 17 · HP 39 · Speed 35 ft · Initiative +2

STR 19 (+4) · DEX 14 (+2) · CON 12 (+1) · INT 5 (−3) · WIS 16 (+3) · CHA 14 (+2)

Key Features. Oath of the Open Seas (Paladin subclass), Lay on Hands (25 HP healing pool per long rest), Channel Divinity — Divine Sense, Fury of the Tides, Marine Layer — Extra Attack, immune to being put to sleep by magic.

Passive Perception 16 · Passive Insight 16 · Passive Investigation 7

Given to Billy by Tide at Crabshack, who said it had belonged to Marilda.

Base

+1 AC. 3 Promise Charges.
Making a Promise. Once per day, Billy can make a promise to the shield. If he keeps it, every spent Promise Charge resets. A promise sticks until Billy stops actually treating it as his priority — and the shield can demand he finish an existing promise before it'll accept a new one.
Window. Pressed flat against a thin wall, it can also work as a one-way window.

Level 3 — Promise of Adventure

(1 charge.) Sacrifices its AC bonus to split into three shards. Thrown up to 30 ft, it splits mid-air to strike up to three targets — or lodges in place to form footholds and obstacles.

Level 4 — Promise of Shelter

(1 charge.) Raised overhead, its shards orbit the head, forming a 10 ft barrier against rain or anything falling from above.

Level 5 — Promise of Protection

(1 charge.) Shards embed in the ground in a 5 ft radius. Hostile Small or Medium creatures caught inside take half damage until Billy drops a creature to 1 HP, or a minute passes.

Level 7 — Promise of Precision

(1 charge.) Thrown up to 20 ft, it splits to strike up to three chosen targets, then orbits them — advantage on attacks against them until one of them is dropped.

Level 9 — Promise of Bubbles

Promise Charges: 4. (1 charge.) Underwater, the shield grows into a 15 ft floor with a rising air bubble above it — protection Billy can move 10 ft at a time.

Level 11 — Promise of the Living

(2 charges.) The shield splits into hundreds of pieces — either shielding a badly hurt ally, or trapping a weakened enemy until Billy releases them or the shield takes enough damage.

Level 12 — Promise of Victory

Promise Charges: 5. (4 charges.) Shatters into glowing shards for one final effect — a wide burst of damage, a temporary armor boost, or one devastating strike on a single target. Whichever Billy chooses, the shield breaks permanently afterward.

Timeline

Outside the inner boundary, every region keeps its own calendar — there's no shared way to line up "when" something happened out there. Inside the inner boundary, though, there's one shared reckoning: the Inner Boundary Calendar (IBC), counted in years since time first started being tracked here. Where a date's given below, it's in IBC years. Time doesn't run at the same speed on both sides of the boundary — sometimes wildly not — so treat these as the crew's best current understanding, not a fixed conversion.
